## Node.js 24 or newer

`@dudousxd/nestjs-authz` 0.11, its 2.x adapters (`-drizzle`, `-typeorm`, `-mikro-orm`, `-prisma`,
`-inertia`, `-testing`, `-codegen`), `-client` 0.3 and `-react` 0.2 require **Node.js 24 or newer**
(`engines.node` is `>=24`). Releases are tested on Node 24 and 26.

Move your app to Node 24 before you upgrade. The APIs are unchanged.
